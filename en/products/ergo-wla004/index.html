WLA004

A dual gas-spring arm for two 17 to 30 inch screens.

This page is based on published information. Deskora has not physically tested this product.

Overview

The WLA004 has two independent gas-spring arms on one pole, each rated for a 2 to 9 kg, 17 to 30 inch monitor. The store lists ABS and aluminium, and the photos show both an edge clamp and a grommet mount. For a two-screen setup, see our dual-monitor guide too.
