Monitor Arms · Ergo

WLA003

A single-monitor pole mount for 17 to 30 inch screens.

This page is based on published information. Deskora has not physically tested this product.

Overview

The WLA003 is a pole mount that clamps to the desk edge, with an arm that slides up and down the pole. The store rates it for 17 to 30 inch monitors up to 10 kg. Height is set by hand rather than by a gas spring.
