Laptop Stands · Nillkin

ProDesk Adjustable

An aluminium laptop stand with adjustable height and angle.

This page is based on published information. Deskora has not physically tested this product.

Overview

Nillkin’s ProDesk is an aluminium desktop stand that lifts a laptop towards eye level, with adjustable height and angle. With a separate keyboard and mouse, it effectively turns the laptop into a monitor (see our laptop ergonomics guide).
