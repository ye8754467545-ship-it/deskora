Anatomy

How to read a good chair

01

The back

A back should follow the natural curve of your spine. Check whether the lumbar support moves or stays fixed.

02

The arms

Arms should hold your forearms without lifting your shoulders. Adjustable height and width matter most.

03

Lift and mechanism

If your feet do not sit flat, measure the seat-height range against your own height.

04

Base and casters

Hard-floor and carpet casters are different. Consider your floor before you order.
