Tidying desk cables, especially on a standing desk

Messy cables do not just look bad. On a standing desk, a cable that is too short gets pulled when the desk rises and can drag a device off the desk or be damaged. Tidying cables is an afternoon’s work.

1. First, take stock

Unplug everything and see where each cable goes. Set aside any you do not use. There are usually more cables than you think.

2. Put the power strip under the desk

Fit a cable tray under the desk and put the power strip in it. Every device then draws power from under the desk, and only one cable runs from the tray down to the wall socket.

3. One route to the floor

Run the main cable down beside a desk leg. Spiral wraps or a cable spine gather several cables into one route.

4. Slack for a standing desk

The most important point: raise the desk to its highest setting and see which cables pull tight. Every cable running from the desk to the floor or wall must still have some slack at full height. Keep cables clear of the moving legs and frame.

5. On the desk, one cable each

For each device on the desk, bring one cable up over the back edge. Reusable hook-and-loop ties make later changes easy.

Safety

  • Do not load power strips and adapters beyond their rating.
  • Keep cables away from chair casters and the desk’s moving parts.
  • Do not enclose warm power adapters where air cannot reach them.

This is general guidance and does not replace advice from a doctor or specialist. If you have persistent pain, speak to a professional.
