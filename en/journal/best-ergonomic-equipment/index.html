Where to start with ergonomic workspace equipment

You do not need to buy everything at once. A simple order usually helps you spend sensibly, and shows you which change really helped.

1. First, adjust what you own

Before any purchase, adjust your current chair, screen height and keyboard position. Sometimes a laptop on a few books and a separate keyboard solves part of the problem today.

2. The screen

If you work on a laptop, a stand with a separate keyboard is a big change. If you have a monitor, an arm also frees up desk surface as well as setting the height.

3. The chair

A chair is usually the largest expense. It makes sense if you sit for hours a day, but read a chair guide before buying.

4. The standing desk

An adjustable desk lets you change position through the day. The goal is not to stand all day. It is to avoid staying in one position for a long time.

5. The details

  • A keyboard and mouse, if your wrists tire after work.
  • Proper lighting, if you work into the evening.
  • A footrest, if your feet do not reach the floor comfortably.

This is general guidance and does not replace advice from a doctor or specialist. If you have persistent pain, speak to a professional.

Sources: OSHA, Computer Workstations eTool
