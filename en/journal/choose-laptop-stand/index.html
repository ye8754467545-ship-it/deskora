How to choose a laptop stand

A laptop’s core problem is that its screen and keyboard are joined. Raise the screen and the keyboard sits too high for typing. Place the keyboard well and your neck bends down. A stand only fully works with a separate keyboard and mouse.

Fixed stands

They suit a permanent desk. They are stable and simply built, but they have no height adjustment, so check the height against your stature and desk. The mStand is an example.

Folding stands

They suit travel, cafés and shared offices. They are light and have several heights, but usually feel less stable when you touch the screen. The Roost is an example.

What to check

  • Does the final height bring the top of the screen near eye level?
  • Are your laptop’s size and weight supported?
  • Does it leave the laptop’s vents clear?
  • Do you have a separate keyboard and mouse?

This is general guidance and does not replace advice from a doctor or specialist. If you have persistent pain, speak to a professional.

Sources: OSHA, Computer Workstations eTool
